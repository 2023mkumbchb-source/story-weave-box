Looking for a Friend in Thika? Sometimes, a Conversation Is a Good Place to Start.

Life can feel heavy sometimes. You may have plenty on your mind but no one you feel comfortable talking to—or you may simply want to meet someone new.

I am also looking to make genuine friends. I am based around Thika, Kenya, and welcome respectful conversations with people from different backgrounds. Whether you study at Mount Kenya University (MKU), have recently moved to Thika, or live elsewhere in Kenya, a friendship can begin with a simple message.

You do not need a special reason to reach out

Maybe you want to talk about your day, school, work, your goals, relationships, or the things you rarely say out loud. Maybe you are new to the area and would like to know someone. Or maybe you just want a light conversation, a laugh, and a break from the usual routine.

You do not have to pretend that everything is fine, and you do not need to have the perfect words. I value respectful, honest conversations and the possibility of building a real friendship over time.

Who is welcome?

  • People looking for friendship in Thika or near Mount Kenya University.
  • Students who want to meet people beyond their usual circles.
  • Anyone in Kenya who would appreciate a friendly conversation.
  • People of any gender and background who value mutual respect.

There is no pressure to become close immediately. Start with a message, introduce yourself if you feel comfortable, and see whether conversation flows naturally.

A small note about safety

Please keep first conversations respectful and avoid sharing passwords, financial details, identity documents, or other sensitive information. If you decide to meet someone in person, choose a public place and tell someone you trust.

Friendship starts with mutual respect, clear boundaries, and no pressure. This is a friendship invitation, not a dating service or emergency support service.
