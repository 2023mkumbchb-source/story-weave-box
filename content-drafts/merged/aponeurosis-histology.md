# Cell Organelles & General Cytology

## Q1: State two functions of the organelles labelled A and B (2+2 marks)

![Electron micrograph of a mitochondrion (A) beside rough endoplasmic reticulum (B)](https://ompathstudy.com/uploads/2026/08/y1-anatomy-pat3-2015-q21-c20823dc.jpg)

**Answer:**
- A — mitochondrion. Two functions:
- ATP production via oxidative phosphorylation
- Site of the citric acid (Krebs) cycle
- B — rough endoplasmic reticulum. Two functions:
- Synthesis of proteins destined for secretion or membrane insertion
- Site of ribosome attachment and initial protein folding
